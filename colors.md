Here’s a professional color palette perfectly matched for a clean, modern poker app like you’re building:

⸻

🎨 Recommended Poker App Color Palette:

Purpose Color Example
Background #0C0C0C (almost black – sleek and premium)
Card/Felt Green #006400 (deep casino green – classic poker table)
Accent Red #C0392B (rich, bold red for “Sign Out”, alerts)
Accent Gold #FFD700 (luxurious gold for highlights, important numbers)
Light Text #F2F2F2 (off-white text color, soft on the eyes)
Secondary Text #A0A0A0 (muted grey for subtext, hints, etc.)
Button Green #27AE60 (fresh green for action buttons like “Create Another League”)
Chip Black #1C1C1C (dark element, chip backgrounds, containers)

⸻

✅ How to use it:

Element Color
App background #0C0C0C
Cards / Chips images use real textures (overlay with light green #006400 behind)
Primary buttons #27AE60 (Green) with white text
Danger buttons #C0392B (Red)
Highlights (stats, big winnings) #FFD700 (Gold)
Titles & important text #F2F2F2
Secondary info, less important labels #A0A0A0

⸻

🎯 Visual feel you’ll get:
• Dark, sleek background
• Rich, vibrant red and gold highlights
• Classic green poker feel without looking “old school”
• Buttons and action items pop out elegantly
